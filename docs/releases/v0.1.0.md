The first release of a native DRTV client for the desktop: one window, DR's own front page, and a
player that plays what DR serves without a CDM.

**Unofficial.** Not made, endorsed or supported by DR. Nothing here ships DR artwork or trademarks;
everything you watch comes from DR's own service under DR's own terms, and outside Denmark most of
the catalogue will refuse to play, because that is DR's decision rather than this app's.

## What works

- **The front page**, in DR's order — including *Se videre* and *Min liste*, read from your profile
  when you are signed in and kept locally when you are not.
- **Search**, grouped the way DR groups it.
- **Title pages**: hero, description, seasons, episodes and the rest of an episode's season.
- **Playback** of DR's clear HLS titles: adaptive resolution, audio and subtitle tracks (Danish
  preferred), scrubbing, skip markers, resume, and end-of-film detection.
- **Sign-in** through DR's device code, so your history and your list follow you. Optional:
  browsing and playback work anonymously, with a local resume point.

## What does not

- **Live television.** The endpoints this client speaks do not expose DR's live channels.
- **PlayReady titles.** Some of the catalogue is DRM-protected and needs a CDM this app does not
  have and will not get.
- **Downloads and casting.**

## Downloads

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `DRTV-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip` | Apple silicon Macs |
| `DRTV-0.1.0-macos-x86_64.zip` | Intel Macs |

Unzip, drag `DRTV.app` wherever you keep applications, and open it. The bundle is ad-hoc signed, not
notarised — there is no Apple developer account behind this — so the first launch needs
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, or:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DRTV.app
```

It carries libmpv and libmpv's entire library closure inside `Contents/Frameworks`, so it runs on a
Mac that has never had Homebrew on it.

**Linux and Windows** build and run from source today (see the README) but have no packaged
download yet: bundling libmpv's closure for Linux and finding an MSVC import library for Windows are
the next pieces of work. CI type-checks both.

## Verifying the download

```sh
shasum -a 256 -c DRTV-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip.sha256
```

## Known gaps

- *Mest sete lige nu* is drawn only when DR decides to inline its items; the recommendation row
  currently comes back empty without a recommendation session.
- Fullscreen defers to the next frame; it is deliberate, not broken.
